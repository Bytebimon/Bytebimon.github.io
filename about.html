About Me

Hello everyone Im Joshua, im a mostly independent game developer working on my very first official game called ByteBimon. ByteBimon is a 2D creature raising game heavily inspired by the Digimon® series, set in a world where players can raise, train, and battle with their own ByteBimon.

The game is currently under development, with plans for multiplayer, a fully developed story, raids, and a variety of ByteBimon creatures for players to collect and battle with. Im passionate about game development and dedicated to creating an engaging and enjoyable experience for all players.

I hope you enjoy the game and I look forward to your feedback and support!

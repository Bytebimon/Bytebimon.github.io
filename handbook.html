Bytebimon Handbook

A complete guide to the desktop apps, story systems, DNA tools, and console commands inside the Byte network.

Quick Start

  • Open the desktop and click the app icons to launch tools such as the browser, battle app, scanner, mission board, log, and splicer.
  • Use the console window to run commands like launch byte.exe or help?.
  • When a rampaging Bytebimon appears, open the Log app and complete the purification mini-game to collect a DNA fragment.
  • Use the Splicer app to fuse a DNA fragment into your current Bytebimon and increase its stats.

Desktop Apps

Byte.exe

The main game window for your Bytebimon experience. Use it to hatch, feed, train, battle, and evolve your current partner.

Web Browser

Browse the in-world web. Enter commands such as binary.load, calculator.com, or bytebimon.com.

Note: the special hack event can appear randomly while browsing. It opens as a full-screen overlay only for that event.

DigitalScanner

This is the adventure scanner. It features a timing mini-game where you lock on at the right moment to collect loot. If you fail on harder difficulties, your stats can drop.

ByteAi

Ask it about missions, story progress, Bytebimon lore, combat, and the current state of the network. It can tell you what missions are active or completed.

Mission Board

Track your story missions. You can mark tasks as complete and keep your progress organized as you play.

Story Archive

Read the ongoing story beats and unlock more of the narrative as the desktop world expands.

Log

This app handles random rampaging Bytebimon alerts. When one appears, you must solve a 4-block sequence puzzle to purify the threat and collect a DNA fragment.

Splicer

Fuse a saved DNA fragment into your current Bytebimon. This gives your monster half of the fragment’s stat values. If you collect three matching DNA fragments, the current monster can evolve.

How the DNA System Works

  • Each successful purification event awards a DNA fragment.
  • Fragments are stored locally in the browser and remain available until you clear them.
  • The Splicer app lets you fuse one fragment into your current Bytebimon.
  • Fusion adds half of the fragment's stats to your monster.
  • Three matching fragments can be used to trigger an evolution path for the current mon.

Console Commands

  • launch byte.exe — opens the main Bytebimon game.
  • launch info — opens the system info window.
  • web — opens the browser.
  • launch battle — opens the battle app.
  • launch scanner — opens the adventure scanner.
  • launch inventory — opens the inventory.
  • launch bytewire — opens the event feed.
  • launch log — opens the Log app.
  • launch splicer — opens the Splicer app.
  • min_ramp — displays the remaining time until the next rampaging Bytebimon alert.
  • help? — opens the story/help flow.

How to Use the Log and Splicer

  1. Keep the desktop open and wait for the alert.
  2. When an alert appears, open the Log app.
  3. Replay the 4-block color sequence in the same order to purify the threat.
  4. Once purified, a DNA fragment is added to your saved collection.
  5. Open the Splicer app and choose a saved fragment.
  6. Fuse it into your current Bytebimon to boost its stats.

Tips

  • Use the scanner and log systems regularly to build your collection of DNA and resources.
  • Save progression by keeping the browser tab open; most progress is stored locally in your browser.
  • If you want a stronger partner, focus on saving multiple fragments and fusing them carefully.
